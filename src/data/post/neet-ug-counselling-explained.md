Qualifying NEET UG is an important step towards becoming a doctor, but it does not guarantee a medical college seat. After the results, candidates must participate in counselling to compete for available MBBS and BDS seats.

In India, NEET UG counselling mainly takes place through two routes: **MCC counselling and state counselling**. Understanding the difference can help students identify suitable colleges, follow the correct registration process and avoid missing important deadlines.

This guide explains the process for the upcoming NEET UG 2027 admission cycle. Since the 2027 counselling bulletin and schedule have not been verified as of 24 November 2026, the detailed process below uses the official 2026 rules as a reference.

## 1. What is NEET UG counselling?

NEET UG counselling is the process through which qualified candidates are considered for admission to medical and dental colleges. Seat allotment depends on factors such as NEET All India Rank, category, eligibility, college preferences and seat availability.

The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducts NEET UG and declares the results. It does not allot college seats.

Two main authorities handle MBBS and BDS counselling:

- **Medical Counselling Committee (MCC):** Handles specified All India Quota seats and seats in central institutions and deemed universities.
- **State counselling authorities:** Handle state quota seats and other seats assigned to them under state admission rules.

## 2. What is MCC counselling?

MCC stands for Medical Counselling Committee. It operates under the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.

MCC conducts online counselling for several categories of medical and dental seats, including:

- 15% All India Quota (AIQ) seats in participating government medical and dental colleges.
- Seats in AIIMS and JIPMER.
- Specified central university seats, including BHU and AMU.
- Seats in other specified central institutions.
- Applicable MBBS and BDS seats in deemed universities, including eligible NRI seats.
- Other institutional seat categories listed in the current counselling bulletin.

Candidates from different states can compete for eligible AIQ seats, subject to the applicable rules.

**Official portal:** https://mcc.nic.in/ug-medical-counselling/

## 3. What is state counselling?

State counselling is conducted by the designated admission authority of each state or Union Territory.

It generally covers:

- State quota seats in government medical colleges.
- Applicable seats in private medical colleges.
- State-specific institutional and other eligible seat categories.

State counselling follows the eligibility, domicile, reservation and admission policies applicable in that state.

For example, Karnataka uses the **Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA)** for its designated medical admission processes.

Students should check the relevant state authority's official notification to understand domicile requirements, category certificates, registration deadlines and the colleges included in its seat matrix.

## 4. MCC vs state counselling: What is the difference?

| Feature                | MCC counselling                                                               | State counselling                                               |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Conducting authority   | Medical Counselling Committee                                                 | Respective state/UT authority                                   |
| Main seat category     | 15% AIQ in participating government colleges and other specified institutions | State quota and other state-controlled seats                    |
| Geographic eligibility | Eligible candidates across India for applicable AIQ seats                     | State-specific eligibility and domicile rules may apply         |
| Central institutions   | Yes, specified institutions                                                   | Generally not the main route                                    |
| Deemed universities    | Applicable seats through MCC                                                  | Only where the notified rules assign seats to the state process |
| Private colleges       | Specified categories, including deemed universities                           | Applicable private-college seats under state rules              |
| Registration           | Separate MCC registration                                                     | Separate state registration                                     |
| Seat allotment         | MCC                                                                           | State counselling authority                                     |

**Important:** The table describes the general division under the 2026 rules. The exact seat categories and participating institutions for 2027 must be checked in the official counselling notices.

## 5. How do NEET counselling rounds work?

Although the exact 2027 schedule is not announced, the following is the general sequence followed in the 2026 counselling process.

### Step 1: Check your NEET result

Download your NEET scorecard from the official NTA website.

Check your All India Rank, category details and qualifying status. Remember that qualifying NEET does not guarantee a seat.

### Step 2: Register for counselling

Register separately on the relevant portals:

- MCC portal for seats handled by MCC.
- Your state counselling portal for state-controlled seats.

Registration for one process does not automatically register you for the other.

### Step 3: Fill in your college preferences

Select the colleges and courses you are interested in, and arrange them in your preferred order.

Research the college location, fees, course availability and seat category before locking your choices.

### Step 4: Seat allotment – Round 1

The counselling authority processes the submitted preferences using the applicable merit, reservation and seat-allocation rules.

Candidates are informed of their allotted college through the official portal.

### Step 5: Accept the allotted seat

Depending on the counselling rules, candidates may need to download the allotment letter, report to the institution, complete document verification and pay the required admission fees.

Check the rules carefully before accepting or declining a seat.

### Step 6: Participate in further rounds

Candidates who do not receive a seat, or who are eligible under the applicable rules for an upgrade, may participate in subsequent rounds.

These may include:

- Round 2
- Round 3
- Stray vacancy round

The number, names and rules of rounds can change. MCC and state authorities publish their own schedules and instructions.

### Step 7: Complete final admission

After receiving a seat, complete all reporting and admission requirements within the official deadline.

Failure to report or comply with the rules can result in cancellation of the allotted seat or other consequences specified in the counselling bulletin.

## 6. Documents generally required for counselling

The exact list depends on the counselling authority and candidate category. Commonly requested documents include:

- NEET UG admit card.
- NEET UG scorecard or rank letter.
- Class 10 certificate or date-of-birth proof.
- Class 12 marksheet and passing certificate.
- Government-issued photo identity proof.
- Recent passport-size photographs.
- Category certificate, where applicable.
- PwBD certificate, where applicable.
- Domicile or residence certificate, if required by the state.
- Other certificates specified in the counselling notice.

Do not assume that every candidate needs every document. Follow the official checklist for your counselling category.

## 7. Counselling fees and college fees

Candidates should distinguish between counselling registration fees, security deposits and college tuition fees.

MCC and state authorities publish their own payment instructions. The amount can depend on the counselling category and type of seat.

**2027 counselling fees: Not announced.**

A verified 2027 fee range is not available as of 24 November 2026. Candidates should not rely on unofficial estimates. Refer to the relevant 2027 counselling bulletin for the exact INR amounts, refund rules and payment deadlines.

College tuition fees are separate from counselling registration fees. Government, private and deemed universities may have substantially different fee structures. Check the official college or state fee notification before filling in preferences.

## 8. Which counselling process should you choose?

Students do not necessarily have to choose only one counselling route.

If eligible, a candidate may register for both MCC counselling and their state counselling process. This can provide access to different categories of seats.

For example, a Karnataka student may consider:

- MCC counselling for eligible AIQ and other MCC-controlled seats.
- KEA counselling for Karnataka state-controlled seats.

However, eligibility for Karnataka state seats depends on the applicable state rules. Being a resident of a state does not automatically establish eligibility for every state quota category.

Always check the relevant eligibility conditions before paying registration or security fees.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

### 1. Can I participate in both MCC and state counselling?

Yes, candidates may register for both processes if they meet the relevant eligibility requirements. Each process has separate registration, choice-filling and seat-allocation rules.

### 2. Is MCC counselling only for government medical colleges?

No. MCC also handles specified seats in central institutions, deemed universities and other notified categories. The exact participating institutions and seat categories are listed in its annual bulletin.

### 3. Does state counselling require a domicile certificate?

It depends on the state and the seat category. Some state quota seats have domicile or other eligibility requirements. Check the official state counselling notification.

### 4. What happens if I do not get a seat in Round 1?

You may be eligible to participate in later rounds, subject to the rules of the relevant counselling authority. Check the current round's eligibility, choice-filling and resignation rules.

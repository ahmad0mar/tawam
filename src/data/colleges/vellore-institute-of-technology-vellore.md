## About Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT Vellore)

Established in 1984 as Vellore Engineering College by Dr. G. Viswanathan, **Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT)** has grown into an Institution of Eminence (IoE) and one of the most sought-after private universities in India. Located in Katpadi, Vellore, Tamil Nadu, VIT is accredited with the highest **NAAC A++ grade** and consistently ranks among the top 15 engineering institutions nationwide in the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF).

The university operates under a Flexible Credit System (FFCS), which allows students to choose courses, faculty, and timetables according to their individual career ambitions and academic pace.

## Key Academic Highlights

- **Flexible Choice-Based Credit System (FFCS):** Tailor course schedules, choose interdisciplinary electives, and balance major-minor specializations.
- **Research Output:** Thousands of Scopus-indexed research papers published annually, supported by nanotechnology and IoT research facilities.
- **Accreditation:** ABET (USA) accreditation for major B.Tech programmes in addition to NAAC A++ and UGC recognition.
- **International Collaborations:** Semester Abroad Programmes (SAP) with over 300 international universities across the US, UK, Europe, and Australia.

## Admission Process & Accepted Entrance Exams

Admission to undergraduate engineering programmes is primarily conducted through the computer-based **VITEEE (VIT Engineering Entrance Examination)** held each spring across India and abroad.

1. **Eligibility:** Candidates must have secured a minimum of 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology in Class 12 (50% for SC/ST and North-Eastern state candidates).
2. **Fee Categorization:** Seats in VITEEE counselling are grouped into Category 1 through Category 5. Early rank holders secure Category 1 tuition rates (₹1.73L–₹1.98L per year), while later ranks can opt for Categories 2 to 5.
3. **BBA & Management Admissions:** Admission to the BBA programme at VIT Business School is based on Class 12 qualifying board exam merit without a separate entrance test.

## Placements & Career Outcomes

VIT holds records in the Limca Book of Records for the highest number of campus placement offers from IT and product companies.

- **Highest Package:** ₹1.02 Crore per annum (International product role)
- **Super Dream & Dream Offers:** Over 3,000+ students receive offers above ₹10 LPA annually.
- **Major Hiring Partners:** Microsoft, Amazon, AppDynamics, DE Shaw, PayPal, Morgan Stanley, TCS Ninja/Digital, and Infosys.

## Campus Life & Hostel Facilities

VIT Vellore features an expansive, self-contained 372-acre campus:

- Separate multi-storeyed hostels for boys and girls with single, 2-bed, 3-bed, 4-bed, and 6-bed configurations.
- Dining catering to South Indian, North Indian, Veg, Non-Veg, and Continental dietary preferences.
- 24/7 security, multi-specialty health centre with ambulances, indoor sports complexes, Olympic-sized swimming pool, and round-the-clock library access.

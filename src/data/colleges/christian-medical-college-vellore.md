## About Christian Medical College (CMC Vellore)

Founded in 1900 by Dr. Ida Sophia Scudder, **Christian Medical College (CMC Vellore)** is an established medical school and teaching hospital in Vellore, Tamil Nadu. CMC is known across India for clinical training, ethical medical practice, and community healthcare. It consistently ranks among the top 3 medical colleges in India alongside AIIMS New Delhi and PGIMER Chandigarh.

The institution includes a 3,000+ bed tertiary multi-specialty healthcare centre catering to patients from all across South Asia and abroad.

## MBBS Academic Programme & Clinical Training

The undergraduate MBBS course at CMC Vellore is affiliated with the Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University and regulated by the National Medical Commission (NMC).

- **Bedside Clinical Exposure:** Students receive hands-on clinical training from the second year in one of India's busiest referral hospitals.
- **Community Health Training:** CMC operates the famous Community Health and Development (CHAD) and Rural Unit for Health and Social Affairs (RUHSA) centres, embedding medical students in rural and primary healthcare delivery.
- **Compulsory Internship:** A 12-month rotating internship in medicine, surgery, obstetrics & gynaecology, paediatrics, and rural health.

## Admission Process & Eligibility

All MBBS admissions to CMC Vellore are processed strictly on the basis of **NEET-UG** scores through the Directorate of Medical Education (DME), Tamil Nadu:

1. **Qualification:** Minimum 50th percentile in NEET UG for general candidates with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology, and English in Class 12.
2. **Counselling:** Candidates must register for Tamil Nadu state medical counselling and submit CMC institutional category verification forms.
